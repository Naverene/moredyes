## GregTech 5 Unofficial: granite textures

The speckled granite and smooth speckled granite textures are GregTech's red granite textures
(`GRANITE_RED_STONE.png` and `GRANITE_RED_SMOOTH.png` from
https://github.com/GTNewHorizons/GT5-Unofficial, `src/main/resources/assets/gregtech/textures/blocks/iconsets/`).
GT5-Unofficial is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later
([licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt](licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt), which builds on [licenses/GPL-3.0.txt](licenses/GPL-3.0.txt)).
These files, and the grey and tinted versions made from them, stay under that license:

- `*/tools/base/speckled_granite.png` (copy of `GRANITE_RED_STONE.png`) and
  `*/tools/base/smooth_speckled_granite.png` (copy of `GRANITE_RED_SMOOTH.png`)
- `1.7.10/src/main/resources/assets/moredyes/textures/blocks/base/speckledGranite.png` and `smoothSpeckledGranite.png`
- the textures the tools make from them: `textures/block/tinted/speckled_granite.png` and
  `smooth_speckled_granite.png` (1.16.5 to 1.20.1), `textures/block/speckled_granite.png` and
  `smooth_speckled_granite.png` (1.21.1 and newer), `textures/blocks/tinted/speckled_granite.png` and
  `smooth_speckled_granite.png` (1.12.2), and tiles 121 and 122 of `1.4.7/src/main/resources/moredyes/terrain.png`

The tools turn them grey and brighten them so the dye color can tint them; the pattern is GregTech's.
